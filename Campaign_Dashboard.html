✅ Paid Push Consistent Winner

Both apps show positive lift on paid user push campaigns. SU Plays Like hit 98.4% confidence at +2.77pp — strongest result in program. Pattern is reliable.

⚠️ Free Push Mixed Results

Free user push underperforms or matches control in both apps. SU Plays Like -0.28pp; GS AST -0.10pp. Likely segment-level effect — free users are more passive.

🔴 SU Email Now 0-for-6

Move To Live Braze email (7/16) makes it six straight with no significant session lift — 17.73% confidence, -0.05pp. Strong 53.74% open rate (one of the best in the program) but still isn't converting to incremental sessions.

🏆 Lance Gill Push — 100% Win

Move To Live iOS push hit 100% confidence at +1.55pp, 32,994 sessions — best feature highlight result since GS Watch-Only (6/27). Android push (24.00% confidence) had much smaller reach and no clear win.

⚠️ Push Key Events Inconsistent Across Platforms

Recurring pattern: on 7/5 Social Sharing push, planned events didn't match what was configured. On 7/18 Lance Gill push, iOS tracked all 3 events (homepage/details/sub) but Android was missing drills_homepage_loaded entirely. Worth a pre-launch checklist to confirm event parity across iOS/Android before each push.

🏆 Strokes Gained (APT) GS iOS Push — Clean Win

99.92% confidence, +0.80pp lift, 63,397 sessions (8/1). Android (AST) counterpart missed on a small sample (+1.22pp, 45.65% confidence).

🔴 Clubhouse Feed — 0-for-4

Missed on every channel tested: Braze email (71.34% conf), CM email (strong 59.96% open, negligible clicks), iOS push (49.70%, negative lift), Android push (26.60%). Worth reconsidering the angle — currently generic feature-promo style rather than a content/story hook.

🔴 Green Reading Maps — 0-for-4

Same pattern as Clubhouse Feed: Braze email (67.36% conf), CM email (59.04% open, low clicks), iOS push (67.85%, negative lift), Android push (pending confidence recheck, but flat +0.11pp). Trial-CTA tier-split mechanic hasn't produced a session win anywhere yet for this feature.

⚠️ GS Conversion Event Drift

Multiple 7/30–8/8 GS sends fired different conversion events than configured/expected — Round_Setup_Started, Round_Setup_Round_Options_Viewed, and Statistics_Category_Selected showed up in place of the documented Facility_List_Viewed / stats_viewed. Flagged across 5+ rows this cycle — worth a batch conversation with Kyle/MaryBeth on whether GS event mapping has drifted from the documented standard.
